Guidelines
Heat Loss Values:
  • Based on actuator specifications
  • Typical motor: 0.5-5 W
  • Typical gearbox: 10-30% of motor
  • FETs: 5-15% of motor power
Thermal Resistance:
  • Lower R = better heat transfer
  • R1-R3: Series thermal path
  • R4: Parallel bypass path
  • Units: Kelvin per Watt (K/W)
