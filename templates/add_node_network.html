What are Node Networks?

Node networks are thermal models represented by discrete nodes connected through thermal resistances, rather than 3D geometry meshes.

Key Components:
  • Nodes: Thermal lumps with capacity and optional heat sources/sinks
  • Connections: Thermal resistances between nodes defining heat flow paths
  • Network: Collection of nodes and connections forming a thermal circuit
Use Cases:
  • Simplified thermal models for complex geometries
  • Reduced-order models for fast simulation
  • Component-level thermal networks
  • Thermal interface modeling
Tip: Start with a simple 2-3 node network to understand the workflow, then expand as needed.
